# Project Dusk — Ideas

A scratchpad for **anything and everything** we might add to Project Dusk. Nothing here is a
promise — it's a place to capture ideas before they graduate to the [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

How to use this file:

- Drop new ideas under the closest heading. Add a heading if none fit.
- Keep each idea to a line or two. Link issues/discussions if one exists.
- When an idea gets committed to a phase, move it to [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

> **AI disclosure:** Project Dusk is 100% AI-coded. See [AI_CODED.md](AI_CODED.md).

---

## Vehicles & Drivers

- New cars with distinct chassis (bikes, big rigs, hovercraft)
- More two-stage / gimmick specials in the spirit of Tick's crane
- Deeper driver backstories, rivalries, and endings
- Per-car handling personalities (drifty, tanky, twitchy)

## Weapons & Combat

- New pickups (lightning arc, shockwave, cluster mine, deployable turret)
- Weapon tiers / upgrades found mid-match
- Combo inputs for special maneuvers
- Environmental kills (shove cars into hazards)

## Arenas & Environment

- More arenas with unique themes and layouts
- Destructible props and cover
- Dynamic hazards (toxic pools, crushers, moving platforms)
- Day/night and weather variants
- Interactive pickups tied to arena geometry

## Game Modes

- Team deathmatch
- Objective modes (capture, king-of-the-hill, escort)
- Survival / horde vs escalating bots
- Time attack / score attack
- Boss encounters

## UI / UX

- Colorblind-friendly palettes
- Fully remappable controls + input glyph switching
- Subtitles and audio captions
- In-game garage to preview cars, specials, and lore
- Photo mode

## Audio & Visuals

- Per-arena music tracks and stingers
- Dynamic music that reacts to match intensity
- More voice barks per driver
- Extra GS-era post effects (scanline presets, dithering options)

## Online & Community

- Spectator + replay system
- Cosmetic unlocks and player banners
- Seasonal ranked resets and rewards
- Community arena/car sharing

## Technical / Tooling

- In-editor arena authoring tools
- Automated smoke tests for match flow
- Performance budget dashboard per quality tier
- Mod support hooks
